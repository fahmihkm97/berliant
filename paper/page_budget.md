# ICST Manuscript Page Budget

Status: provisional until the ICST 2027 full submission instructions
are published.

Working format assumption based on ICST 2026:

- IEEE conference two-column format
- 10 pages main paper
- up to 2 additional reference-only pages

## Priority Budget

| Section | Priority |
|---|---|
| Abstract | Keep concise |
| Introduction | Reduce |
| Related Work | Reduce strongly |
| Research Questions | Keep concise |
| Methodology | Protect |
| Experimental Setup | Reduce |
| Results | Protect |
| Discussion | Reduce |
| Limitations | Keep concise |
| Conclusion | Reduce strongly |

## Figures

Keep:

1. Holdout recovery
2. Method recovery comparison
3. Execution comparison
4. Scaling
5. Scaling reduction

Possible consolidation:

- Figures 4 and 5 may be combined later if page pressure is high.

## Tables

Priority:

1. Holdout results
2. Method comparison
3. Ablation
4. Scaling

Sensitivity results can be summarized in text if page pressure is high.

## Cutting Principles

Do not remove:

- unseen-holdout methodology;
- rare PAIR-002 miss;
- baseline comparison;
- ablation evidence;
- confidence intervals;
- synthetic-evaluation limitations;
- novelty distinction from prior work.

Reduce first:

- repeated explanation of SCIF stages;
- repeated numerical results;
- broad motivation;
- lengthy literature descriptions;
- redundant interpretation after tables;
- implementation details reproducible from artifact.
